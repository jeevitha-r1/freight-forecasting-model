# PROJECT TITLE

Freight Intelligence & Vessel Charter Optimization Platform

# PROBLEM

Bulk cargo procurement for India's East Coast ports currently relies heavily on daily freight-market exploration and repeated spot chartering. This creates reactive decision-making, makes it difficult to identify suitable market-entry windows, and can result in inefficient vessel utilization and higher idle time.

The system should help logistics/chartering managers move from repeated individual spot contracts toward data-driven short-term and medium-term multiple-voyage contracts.

The platform should combine:

* Historical freight rates
* Freight-rate forecasts
* Vessel characteristics
* Origin and destination port constraints
* Cargo requirements
* Port congestion
* Commodity and economic indicators
* Vessel feasibility
* Idle-time estimation
* Contract cost scenarios
* Risk indicators

The frontend must present these insights clearly and allow a user to enter a cargo requirement and obtain a complete chartering analysis.
